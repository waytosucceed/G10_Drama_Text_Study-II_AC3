Key Takeaways


  1. What is an aside?
    We know that an aside is a short speech or remark that a character makes to the audience, or to themselves, which the other characters on stage do not hear. In a script, it is marked with the stage direction “(aside)”.

  2. Who hears an aside, and who does not?
    The audience hears every word of an aside, while the other characters on stage hear nothing. The audience therefore knows more about the character than anyone standing beside them.

  3. What is a soliloquy?
    We know that a soliloquy is a speech in which a character, alone on stage, speaks their thoughts and feelings aloud so that the audience can hear them.

  4. What is the difference between an aside and a soliloquy?
    An aside is short and spoken while other characters are on stage and do not hear it. A soliloquy is usually longer and spoken when the character is alone on stage.

  5. Why do playwrights use soliloquies?
    We know that soliloquies reveal a character’s truest thoughts, fears and plans, and show them struggling with a decision. With nobody else present, the character has no reason to hide anything.

  6. What is dramatic irony?
    Dramatic irony is when the audience knows something important that a character on stage does not.

  7. What does dramatic irony do to an audience?
    Dramatic irony creates tension because the audience fears what will happen. It also makes a character’s innocent words carry a second, darker meaning.

  8. Why does the timing of an entrance matter?
    We know that a playwright decides not only who enters, but exactly when. A well-timed entrance can create dramatic irony and tension without a single extra word.

  9. What can an exit do in a play?
    We know that an exit changes who is on stage. When other characters leave, a character can be left alone, which makes a soliloquy possible


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Doughty
Meaning: Brave, courageous, and determined, especially in the face of difficulty or danger.
Example: The doughty explorer pressed onward despite the harsh weather and dwindling supplies.

Sibyls
Meaning: Women believed to possess prophetic powers; female prophets or seers.
Example: In the ancient legend, the sibyls foretold the fall of kingdoms and the rise of new empires.

Erstwhile
Meaning: Former; belonging to an earlier time.
Example: The erstwhile champion returned to the arena after years of retirement.

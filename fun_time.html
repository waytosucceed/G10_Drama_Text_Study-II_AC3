Proverbs on “Crow”

“A hard act to follow”: Someone or something so impressive that whoever comes next will struggle to match it.
Example: Last year’s head girl gave such a brilliant farewell speech that she was a hard act to follow.

“Caught in the act”: Discovered at the very moment of doing something wrong.
Example: My brother was caught in the act of eating the cake meant for the guests, with icing still on his fingers.
